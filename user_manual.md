# User Manual: Camemark Marketplace

Welcome to Camemark! This guide will help you navigate our enchanted marketplace, manage your finances, and connect with producers across Cameroon.

## 1. Getting Started

### Creating an Account
1. Go to the **Signup** page.
2. Choose your primary role: **Buyer**, **Seller**, or **Farmer**.
3. Complete the registration form and verify your email.

### Completing Your Profile (KYC)
To unlock full features like card generation and higher transaction limits:
1. Go to your **Buyer Dashboard**.
2. Open **Profile Settings**.
3. Fill in your details (Nationality, Occupation, Address).
4. Upload a clear photo of your **ID Card or Passport**.
5. Our team will review and verify your account.

## 2. Shopping the Marketplace

### Browsing & Searching
- **Market Zone**: The main hub for all products.
- **Categories**: Filter items by type (Food, Tech, Crafts, etc.).
- **Economic Blocs**: Use the **Regions Map** to shop products specifically from one of Cameroon's 10 regions.
- **Bargain Deals**: Look for the "Bargain" badge to find products with special discounts.

### Placing an Order
1. Select a product to view details.
2. Click **Buy Now** or **Add to Cart**.
3. Choose your delivery address.
4. Select **CamRency Wallet** for the fastest payment.

## 3. Financial Tools (CaMark Wallet)

### Managing Your Wallet
Access your wallet from the Dashboard to:
- **Top Up**: Add funds using local payment methods (Mobile Money, etc.).
- **Send Money**: Instantly transfer funds to other Camemark users.
- **Withdraw**: Move your earnings or balance to your bank or mobile wallet.

### Generating a Visa Card
Need to shop online outside of Camemark?
1. In the Dashboard, click **Create Card**.
2. Choose between a **Virtual Card** (instant) or a **Physical Card** (delivered).
3. Confirm the fee payment from your wallet.
4. Your card details will be securely displayed in your dashboard.

## 4. Tracking Your Orders
- **Active Orders**: View the real-time status of your purchases.
- **Deliveries**: Track your package from the seller to your doorstep.
- **Notifications**: Receive alerts for every step of your order's journey.

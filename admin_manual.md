# Admin Manual: Camemark Command Center

This manual is for Camemark Administrators to manage platform users, monitor sales, and oversee the verification process.

## 1. Accessing the Admin Panel
The **Admin Panel** is located at `/admin`. Only users with the `admin` or `super_admin` role can access this interface.

## 2. Overview Dashboard
The main dashboard provides a high-level view of platform health:
- **Total Users**: Overall growth of the user base.
- **Active Sellers**: Total count of registered Sellers and Farmers.
- **Marketplace Sales**: Total transaction volume in XAF.
- **Pending Approvals**: The number of users waiting for KYC verification.

## 3. User Management
Found under the **User Management** tab:
- **Search**: Find users by name or email.
- **Profile Review**: Click on a user to view their full profile, including their uploaded ID for KYC.
- **Verification**: Toggle the "Verified" status once you have confirmed a user's identity.
- **Role Assignment**: Upgrade or downgrade users between roles (Buyer, Seller, Farmer, Admin).

## 4. Roles & Permissions
The **Roles & Permissions** tab allows for fine-grained control over what different staff members can do on the platform. Use this to ensure the right people have the right access level.

## 5. Sales & Marketplace Oversight
Monitor the flow of goods and money:
- Track total sales trends.
- Oversee pending deliveries and ensure orders are being fulfilled.
- Manage any disputes or issues reported by buyers or sellers.

## 6. System Settings
Configure global platform parameters, such as:
- Marketplace fees.
- Regional settings.
- Payment gateway configurations.
